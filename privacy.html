Life RPG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Life RPG is a personal habit-tracking app. This page explains what data it collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Email address and password — used to create your account and sign you in. Passwords are handled by our backend provider, Supabase, and are never visible to us in plain text.
  • Your habit and goal data — the habits, goals, streaks, and progress you enter into the app, so it can be saved and synced across your devices.
  • Bug reports — if you choose to submit one via Settings → Report a Bug, we store the message text along with your account ID and the time it was sent.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties
  • We don't run ads or third-party analytics/tracking in the app
  • We don't collect any data beyond what's listed above

Where it's stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, our backend and authentication provider. You can also use the app fully offline without an account — in that case, your data stays only in your device's local storage and nothing is sent to our servers.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email burkhardt.simon@gmx.de and it will be removed.

Children's privacy

Life RPG is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: burkhardt.simon@gmx.de
